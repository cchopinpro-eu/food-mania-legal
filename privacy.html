Privacy Policy

Food Mania Tycoon · Last updated: November 17, 2026

Who we are

Food Mania Tycoon (the “Game”) is published by Digital-Play, an independent game studio based in France (“we”, “us”). For any privacy question, contact us at contact@digi-nord.eu.

In short

  • No account is required and we never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device and, if you sign in to Google Play Games, in your Google cloud save.
  • The Game uses third-party services for ads (Google AdMob), purchases (Google Play Billing), achievements, leaderboards and cloud save (Google Play Games Services) and anonymous gameplay analytics (GameAnalytics).
  • All data sent over the network is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

Advertising: Google AdMob

The Game shows ads (interstitial ads and optional rewarded videos) through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address (used to infer approximate location), device and app information, and ad interactions, in order to deliver, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we ask for your consent with Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) before any personalised ads are shown. If you refuse, you only receive non-personalised (contextual) ads, which may still use data for frequency capping, fraud prevention and aggregated measurement. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy option in the Game’s settings (where available in your region). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Google › Ads). Buying “Remove ads” stops interstitial ads. More information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In-app purchases: Google Play Billing

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive your payment details. We only receive the information needed to deliver your purchase (product, purchase token, order status) and to restore it on your device.

Google Play Games Services

If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, Google provides us with your Play Games player ID and gamer name so the Game can show achievements and leaderboards (total money earned, customers served, progress) and keep a cloud save of your progress. Your gamer name and scores may be visible to other players on leaderboards, according to your Play Games profile settings. Google’s privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.

Analytics: GameAnalytics

We use GameAnalytics to understand how the Game is played and to fix problems. It receives anonymous gameplay events (for example sessions, progression, in-game economy, errors), a random identifier generated for the Game, device model, operating system and version, and country derived from the IP address. It does not receive your name or contact details. More information: gameanalytics.com/privacy.

Notifications

The Game may schedule local reminders on your device (for example when your offline income is ready or a daily gift is available). They are created on the device; no data is sent to us. You can turn them off in your Android settings.

Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Personalised advertising: your consent (you can withdraw it at any time).
  • Non-personalised ads, analytics and fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in funding, operating and improving a free game.
  • Purchases and cloud save: performance of the service you request.

Retention

Local data stays on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear its data. Analytics data is kept in pseudonymous form for a limited period (at most 2 years) and then deleted or aggregated. Google keeps data under its own retention rules.

Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the Game and data was collected, contact us at contact@digi-nord.eu and we will delete it.

Your rights and data deletion

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data, and to data portability. To request deletion of the data linked to the Game, follow the steps on the data deletion page or email contact@digi-nord.eu. We answer within 30 days. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France: CNIL, www.cnil.fr).

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). We do not sell your personal data.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version; important changes will be announced in the Game.
